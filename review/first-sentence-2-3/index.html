TouchBebe 2.3.0: first-sentence recording

AccessibilityService declaration review for com.yuni.touchbebe. This optional feature turns a caregiver's explicit Bixby care statement into a record for the baby they selected and consented to. It is not an accessibility tool for users with disabilities (isAccessibilityTool=false).

Access, interpretation and saving

The service listens only to com.samsung.android.bixby.agent and reads its current visible user-input text node. It requires a fresh listening/input → current sentence → processing transition. Old conversation content and Bixby's answer are not commands. It does not click, perform gestures, control other apps, or initiate Bixby.

A bundled, trained character n-gram/TF-IDF classifier selects from a finite list of care record types. Human-written validation checks the addressed baby, completed-event intent, negation/questions, quantities, times and ambiguity. A clear command is saved without an additional confirmation, as disclosed before opt-in; ambiguous input is not saved. This is local command interpretation, not a generative agent. The app does not create goals, independently initiate tasks or plan arbitrary actions.

Consent is bound to the selected baby, spoken alias, lock setting and installed Bixby version. Both consent and Android accessibility permission are required. The app rechecks authorization immediately before writing. The result screen reports local saving and offers cancellation of the exact unchanged records for five minutes. Duplicate commands are guarded. The feature can be switched off in the app or in Android settings.

Disclosure translation

  • Accessibility reads Bixby's current recognized sentence for the phone's local model and validation rules. Other apps' screens are not read or manipulated.
  • A clear care statement addressed to the configured nickname or “baby” is saved to the currently selected baby without another question. Unclear target, amount or time is not saved. Recognition errors can occur; check the result.
  • TouchBebe does not retain or transmit the raw sentence or audio. Derived feeding, diaper, burping and sleep records are stored in the app and synchronize through existing family sharing when configured. This health-related derived data is declared. Bixby's own voice processing follows Samsung's settings.
  • Declining does not prevent manual entry or ordinary in-app voice recording. Switching off stops reading and automatic recording. Changes to baby, alias, lock permission or Bixby version require settings confirmation. Lock-screen recording/result disclosure is separately optional.
